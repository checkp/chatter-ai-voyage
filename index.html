RoboHeard — Agentic AI Orchestration Platform

RoboHeard is an agentic AI orchestration platform that runs a Conductor across eight 2026 frontier models — OpenAI GPT-5, Anthropic Claude 4, Google Gemini 2.5, xAI Grok-4, DeepSeek-R2, Mistral Large, Perplexity Sonar, and Qwen — so one prompt is delegated, debated, and synthesized into a single grounded answer.

What is RoboHeard?

RoboHeard is a multi-model AI chat platform where a Conductor model routes each request to the best-suited frontier model, has them debate when answers disagree, and returns a single synthesized response with citations. It replaces juggling six AI subscriptions with one orchestrated workspace.

How does Conductor Mode work?

Conductor Mode analyzes your prompt, picks a panel of frontier models suited to the task, dispatches sub-queries in parallel, resolves conflicts between their answers, and synthesizes a final response. You see each agent's contribution and the Conductor's reasoning — nothing is hidden.
